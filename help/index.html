Help

You can navigate this website with some vim-style key bindings.

j scroll down a line
k scroll up a line
G scroll to the bottom of page
g g scroll to the top of page
h open home page
r open resume page
p open projects page
b open blog
c open contact page
? open (this) help page

Also ⇥ and ⏎ are your friend for on page navigation.
