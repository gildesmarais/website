Today marks the day I deleted Whatsapp from my phone. From now on you cannot reach me there.

This was a long-running undertaking. Sadly that app is the only messenger a few of my contacts still use.
It's not easy to convince them to install _another_ messenger app.[^1]
I told all remaining contacts where they can find me and sent them the URL to my website.

At some point you have to pull the plug.

## A few reasons for that decision

The reasons are diverse, but these are enough for me:

Whatsapp is owned by Facebook. Even though they said they will not merge Facebook and Whatsapp data, why should I believe that corporation? I do not feel that Facebook cares about privacy.[^2]

And, in my opinion, privacy always was an afterthought on Whatsapp.[^3]

If you think the security story looks better, browse this [dedicated page on Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reception_and_criticism_of_WhatsApp_security_and_privacy_features). It lists security and privacy incidents since 2011.

However, it misses the latest hack. [Read about that in this Guardian article](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2020/jan/21/amazon-boss-jeff-bezoss-phone-hacked-by-saudi-crown-prince). This is not science fiction.
