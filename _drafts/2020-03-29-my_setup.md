## Working environment

I prefer a  Macbook (Pro or a full spec Air) as my main working machine. With such machine I can work temporarily when not being at a fully equipped desk.

A fully equipped desk contains:

- a screen with 2560×1440px (WQHD) resolution with 109dpi minimum
- the <q>old</q>  trackpad
- the <q>old</q>  wireless keyboard with "International Layout"
- a laptop stand to move the machine above the layer where fluids are placed
- a desk-pad

Usually I'm wearing a Bose QC15 headphone with Airmod for bluetooth connectivity. The headphones are with me for seven years. I use them daily, at least 6 hours a working day. They are in a good state without any defects. Some parts wear out, so I've replaced the ear cushions multiple times and the headband once with after market parts for a few bucks. They are super comfortable and run with one AAA batteries (!).

To keep my thoughts or prototype a UI, I rely on a DIN A5 sketchbook (with at least 150g/m<sup>2</sup> paper) and a mechanical pencil with 0.5mm B lead.

## Reducing interruptions

Working while being interrupted kills any flow. Asking <q>may I interrupt you</q> is a meta-question and already an interruptions.

I turned of all system sounds of macOS and disabled the notification center.

Notifications on my phone make no sound or vibration, nor turn on its display. My watch vibrates when it receives notifications of few important people in my life.

## Getting into the flow

I listen to electronic music mixes. A favorite source of fresh music
is the BBC Radio 1 Essential Mix. Depending on my mood on the day, I choose one of ~20 all-time favorites.
I regularly listen to the same mixes. The purpose is to have zero surprise moments while getting into **the flow**. I also find Pete Tong's voice introducing every mix very soothing (I've you're reading this, Pete: Heya 👋).


## Reducing eye and arm strains

Touching the trackpad to move the cursor is a flaw I try to avoid at any time. It's time-intensive and puts a strain on your wrist. Keeping the hands at the keyboard gives you all the power you'd ever want.
Well, as long as you don't develop some GUI and need to arrange that element a few
pixels to the left and align it relative to that element's baseline.

I stand up every hour for a few minutes. My watch reminds me to. I then move the cursor to the bottom left corner. I've setup a 'hot corner' in macOS to lock itself. When returning it unlocks automatically when my watch is close by.
Look outside (focus something in the distance) and make some exercises.

swirl your arm, circle your head, raise/lower your shoulders, hold yourself on the pull-up bar, do some pull-ups.
